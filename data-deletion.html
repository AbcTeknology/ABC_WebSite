Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your ABC AI account and all associated data at any time. This applies whether you signed in with email, Google, Facebook, or Microsoft.

How to request deletion

1

Email privacy@abcteknology.com from the email address associated with your ABC AI account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

2

If you signed in with Google, Facebook, or Microsoft and don't have a separate email on file, tell us which provider you used and the name on your profile so we can locate your account.

3

We confirm your identity, then permanently delete your account and data within 30 days. We'll send a confirmation email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, linked sign-in providers)
  • Saved preferences and shopping baskets
  • Chat and search history
  • Push notification tokens for your devices

Removing ABC AI from your Facebook account only

If you just want to stop ABC AI from accessing your Facebook profile — without deleting your ABC AI account — go to Facebook's Settings & Privacy → Apps and Websites, find "ABC AI", and remove it. This revokes Facebook's connection immediately; your ABC AI account and data remain unless you also complete the deletion request above.

Anything we keep

Minimal, legally required only

Records we're required to retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, transaction logs where required by law) are kept only as long as legally necessary and are not used for any other purpose after your account is deleted.

Questions about this process?

Contact us at privacy@abcteknology.com and we'll help directly. See also our full Privacy Policy.
