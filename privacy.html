Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ABC Teknology ("we", "our", "us") operates the ABC AI Shopping mobile application and this website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how account deletion works, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information we collect

When you use ABC AI Shopping, we collect information you provide directly, including your name and email address when you create an account or sign in with Google, Facebook, or Microsoft. We process your delivery location, chat prompts, searches, baskets, product selections, notification token, app platform, and device identifier used for session and notification delivery. We do not collect payment-card information; purchases are completed directly through the selected retailer.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to authenticate your account, find stores and prices available near your delivery location, generate and refine shopping baskets, maintain your chat history, send requested service notifications, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, respond to support requests, and improve search and recommendation quality. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Data sharing

Information is processed by service providers used to operate ABC AI Shopping, including cloud hosting and database providers, authentication providers selected by you, Google Maps and Places for location features, Expo and platform notification services, and AI/search infrastructure used to produce shopping results. These providers process data on our behalf under their applicable terms. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

4. Data retention and deletion

We retain identifiable account information while your account is active. When account deletion completes, we permanently delete your authentication identity, email and profile links, linked sign-in records, authentication sessions, saved location and preferences, device identifiers, and push-notification tokens. We may retain de-identified chat messages, searches, baskets, product results, and performance records for research and service improvement. Retained research records receive a new random identifier, have direct identifiers removed, and have no stored mapping back to your deleted account. Information that cannot be safely de-identified is deleted. Limited security or legal records may be retained only where required and for no longer than necessary. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Security

We use encrypted HTTPS connections in transit, access controls, token-based authentication, and restricted service credentials. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and tracking

Our app does not use cookies. Our website may use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Third-party sign-in

If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or Microsoft, we receive the account identifier and available profile details needed to create or link your ABC AI Shopping account. We never receive your password for those services. You can revoke provider access from that provider's account settings.

8. Children

ABC AI Shopping is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at privacy@abcteknology.com.
